Hi, Mabuhay! I am a Graphic Designer and Front-End Developer based in Manila, Philippines.

I have a strong passion for design that began in childhood and continues to shape my creative journey today. Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, I combine technical knowledge with artistic skills to create visually engaging and functional digital experiences.
